Project title: NIYAM: Verified LLM Translation of Indian Building Rules into openBIM Checks
Theme: AI, Automation and Digital Workflows

NIYAM ingests 500 clauses from two Indian building bye-laws (445 MBBL 2016, 55 Telangana 2012) and has turned
27 of their sentences into tested rule units. A verifier gate checks every LLM-drafted rule against test
buildings before use: in our headline run (two open-weight models, 415 model calls) it cut the share of
wrong rules shipped from 27% and 41% without the gate to 3% and 0% with it. A draft rulepack of 6 rules
already audits IFC4 models end to end and exports JSON, an HTML report and BCF 2.1 issues. NIYAM is a
decision aid, not a legal approval tool; no human expert has reviewed the rules yet.
