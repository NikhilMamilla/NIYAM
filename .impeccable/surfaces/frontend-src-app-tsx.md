## Scope

Landing page at `/` (Persuade, with Read passages) and dashboard at `/app` (Operate). One world for both.
Audience: civil engineers and plan-scrutiny officers, jurors, modellers. Job: understand what NIYAM does and how far
its results can be trusted, then try it on a sample building. Primary action: "Open the app"; secondary "Try a sample
building". Proof: real run numbers from site-data.json, one real gate rejection, the 3D viewer. Constraints: offline,
no invented geometry, every number from committed data, honest caveats beside every number.

## Direction contract

FORM: Diazo whiteprint (blue-line print) of an architect's submission drawing: warm cream print paper, lines in diazo
blue-violet, deep ink for text, title-block and grid-bubble conventions of Indian plan sheets. seed:59718ec227b1f266.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The page is the print sheet. A whiteprint title block holds the promise, status badge and the two
actions; the hero figure is an isometric line drawing of a small building whose room outlines print in diazo blue and
whose verdict marks (red/amber, with icons) land on specific rooms and doors, captioned as a schematic, not a model.

VISITOR PATH: Sheet sections read like drawing sheets A-01, A-02...: problem, what NIYAM is (pipeline as a drawn
schedule), the gate (stations with the real rejection as a test strip), two kinds of rules, evaluation (figures with
Wilson intervals and n stamped on each figure), Telangana, real-model reality check, demo, standards, limits, team.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The print develops. As a sheet scrolls in, its line work develops from faint to full diazo blue
(stroke reveal, staggered along the drawing order), the way a print emerges from the machine; with reduced motion the
lines are simply fully printed.

CROSS-SURFACE REACH: The dashboard is the same print room at work: paper panels with title-block headers, ink type, the
same verdict grammar; the 3D stage is the dark light-table where the model sits under a warm lamp.

HONEST RISK: Cream ground with a serif-adjacent journal voice is close to the AI default look; the whiteprint line
discipline, the title-block grid and the diazo blue (not terracotta) must carry it, or it becomes generic.

RAISES: provenance stamp on every figure (raku: exact records); pipeline advances stage by stage (cape: named states);
gate shown as stations with a test strip (darkroom); one state grammar: pressed = inverted ink, disabled = half-tone
(one-bit desktop); dashboard regions fixed, contents swap (PC-98); captions hand off the caveat between sheets (comic).
Verdicts: all six challengers declined (lose audience identification and product clarity).
